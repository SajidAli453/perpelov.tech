Contact

You can contact the owner of this site by email at:

sajid453x@gmail.com

Please write in plain, simple language and say which guide or tool your message is about. If a step on a guide did not match your phone, please say which phone model you have, which One UI version you see in Settings if you know it, and the exact step where you got stuck. That detail helps the owner understand the problem and improve the guide.

Please do not send passwords, one-time codes, bank details, or other private information by email. This site cannot help with lost passwords, locked accounts, bank accounts, or phone repairs, and it cannot give personal advice about a specific phone problem by email. For help with an account, a payment, or a repair, please contact Samsung support or your bank directly, using a number or address you already trust.

Messages are read by the site owner when time allows. There is no phone number and no live chat for this site. Thank you for writing, and thank you for using Galaxy Phone Help.

Stop and check

A real bank or phone company will not rush you, ask for passwords or codes, or ask you to move money. If a call or message pressures you, hang up and check another way. Try the Scam Call/Message Checker.
